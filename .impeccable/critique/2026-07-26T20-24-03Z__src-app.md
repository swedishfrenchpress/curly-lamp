# Design Specificity Verdict

**Strongly authored for this campaign — with one category-interchangeable module in the worst possible position, and a conversion wired to placeholders.**

## LLM assessment

This is not a template, and the evidence of authorship is structural rather than decorative. `ui.tsx:11-33` renders citations as publisher names instead of `[1][2]`, with a comment explaining that the distinction between a riksdag document and a newspaper *is the site's method*. `layout.tsx:41-43` ships a `<noscript>` block forcing `.reveal` visible, reasoning that a page about state surveillance will be read by people with JavaScript disabled. DESIGN.md's Long Compound Rule — Swedish sets the minimum width, "Ansiktsigenkänning" runs ~200px at the panel's serif step — is a constraint no generic system would encode, and globals.css:1306-1317 actually sizes a grid track against it. The share panel refuses per-network buttons with a documented reason. The site refuses to solicit tips. These are decisions, not defaults.

The strongest authored composition is the `STÄLLD` / `SVARET` exchange grid on `/sv/questions/` (globals.css:979-1024): the serif question in columns 1-4, beside it two hairline-divided paper cells holding the question as filed and the minister's reply as given. That layout exists *because of this campaign's specific argument* — the gap between a question and a non-answer — and nothing else could ship it unchanged.

**Where it goes category-interchangeable:** the four-number chapter. DESIGN.md's own Four Numbers spec says "Treat the figures as four horizontal evidence records, not a 2×2 metric dashboard… **Do not use vertical dividers**." The base CSS at globals.css:741-775 implements that correctly. Then 2592-2610 overrides it into a bordered four-up grid of 340px cards with vertical dividers, and 2617-2649 adds dithered gold→pink→violet→teal gradient bars. Swap "5 år" for "5× faster" and this ships in a Series-B SaaS landing page. It is the *first* module after the hero, and it triggers two of PRODUCT.md's four anti-references.

The gradient bars are worse than generic — they are semantically corrosive. DESIGN.md reserves gold/rose/violet for the atmospheric field and states they "never color evidence text or factual status," and reserves one civic teal for *unanswered questions*. The fourth bar (globals.css:2643-2648) spends that teal decoratively. On a site whose argument rests on "teal = still unanswered," the signal colour is burned as ornament fifteen hundred pixels before the reader learns what it means.

**Three cheap missed opportunities for character:**
1. **Nothing is individually citable.** A site built to be quoted has no permalink on any question or timeline entry.
2. **The `OBESVARAD` flag never accumulates.** It appears five times and never totals — no "5 av 5 obesvarade", no elapsed-time dateline. The one campaign-specific status device is a label, not an argument.
3. **`src/content/sources.ts` already holds every source with publisher, title, date and URL — and there is no source register page.** DESIGN.md describes mono as "a filing system"; the filing system exists in the data layer and is never shown. Near-zero cost, and exactly what the journalist audience wants.

# Overall Impression

The thinking here is better than the shipping. Read the source and you find a designer arguing with themselves in comments about JS-disabled readers, about why citations name publishers instead of numbering them, about why the share panel deliberately has no LinkedIn button. Read DESIGN.md and you find a 428-line contract with rules — the Long Compound Rule, the Earned Hairline Rule — that no generic system would ever contain. The Strömmer pull quote does the campaign's whole job by doing nothing: his actual sentence, unedited, beside the documentary portrait, and the gap speaks for itself.

Then three things undercut it, all of them mechanical rather than conceptual.

The typography specified in DESIGN.md never renders. The share panel — the campaign's stated conversion, the destination of every share affordance on the site — renders three empty gray rectangles over six real PNGs sitting on disk. And the four-number chapter, the first module a reader meets after the hero, overrides its own committed spec into the exact SaaS dashboard the design document forbids.

**The single biggest opportunity:** the gap between the documented design and the shipped one is almost entirely closable by deletion. Delete six lines in `layout.tsx` and the whole type system appears. Delete fifty-eight lines in `globals.css` and the spec-correct stats module underneath is revealed. Add one `<Image>` and the conversion works. This site is closer to excellent than the score suggests — it just isn't currently running its own design.

# What's Working

**1. The `STÄLLD` / `SVARET` exchange grid** (globals.css:979-1024). Two paper cells divided by a 1px hairline, question in columns 1-4, exchange in 5-12. It works because it makes the reader perform the comparison themselves: here is the question as filed, here is what came back. No adjective does that work. This is why `/sv/questions/` is the best page on the site.

**2. The citation system's refusal to abbreviate** (ui.tsx:11-33, globals.css:922-939). Publisher names, never `[3]`. Then the touch treatment grows the 20px target with a transparent `::after` expander rather than padding, so the mono line and its hairline underline keep exact metrics and only the row gap opens. That is someone protecting a typographic decision under a genuine ergonomic constraint instead of abandoning one for the other.

**3. The honesty architecture as a designed system, not a disclaimer.** The scope statement, the data-map caption stating it is not a technical schema of the Swedish installation, the timeline entry admitting the start year is inferred, the `<noscript>` reasoning. Together these make the nonpartisanship *visible* rather than claimed — which is precisely what converts a sceptical Swedish reader from "this is activism" to "this is a record I can pass on."

## [P0] The share panel renders empty placeholders over six campaign PNGs that already ship

**What.** `share-assets.tsx:35-39` renders `<CampaignImageSlot>` for each preview. `campaign-image-slot.tsx:107-120` contains **no image element at all** — it is a caption-only gray box. Meanwhile the real files are on disk and in the static export: `public/share/vem-svarar-acus-{landscape,square,story}-sv.png` (60/79/97 KB) and the three `-en` equivalents. The correct `href` is already in scope on the very next line (`share-assets.tsx:40`). `next/image` is already imported in six other files.

**Why it matters.** Sharing is PRODUCT.md's stated primary conversion; downloading these assets is the secondary. Every share affordance on the site — the hero "Dela" button, the masthead DELA, and the terminal block at the foot of every section page — routes to this panel. A persuaded reader who scrolls 8,054px to act cannot see what they are about to post. It is also the last content before the footer, so the peak-end rule applies at full force: the final impression of a campaign about institutional non-answers is a section that looks unfinished.

**Fix.** In `share-assets.tsx:35-39`, replace the slot with the real image:
```tsx
<Image src={asset.href} alt={asset.label} width={spec.width} height={spec.height}
       className="share-assets__preview" />
```
Keep `campaign-image-slot.tsx` for the one genuinely unsupplied artwork (the Acus 3:2 explainer). Retain the existing `max-width: 190px` story cap at globals.css:2913-2915.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

## [P1] Geist Sans and Geist Mono never render — anywhere, on any page, since the first commit

**What.** `layout.tsx:28-33` sets an inline style on `<html>`:
```
"--font-geist-sans": "var(--font-geist-sans)",
"--font-geist-mono": "var(--font-geist-mono)",
```
A custom property whose value references itself is invalid at computed-value time, and the inline style outranks the `.variable` classes `geist/font` puts on the same element. globals.css:80-81 then builds `--font-sans` and `--font-mono` on top of those now-invalid properties, so both collapse. Measured in-browser: `--font-geist-sans` computes to `""`, `document.fonts` lists `GeistSans`/`GeistMono` as **`unloaded`**, and `body` resolves to the system stack. Removing the inline style at runtime restores `--font-geist-sans` → `"GeistSans", "GeistSans Fallback"`. `git log -S` confirms these six lines have been present since commit `3bbf8fe` — the initial commit. Baskerville is unaffected; it is a local face, which is why the display type looks right and masks the problem.

**Why it matters.** DESIGN.md commits three type families and says Geist Mono "functions like a filing system for dates, sources, statuses." Every body paragraph, every source label, every date, every status flag, and every mono control on this site currently renders in whatever the reader's OS supplies. The metrics differ from Geist, so measures, line lengths, and the Long Compound Rule's carefully-sized tracks have all been tuned against the wrong font. This is the single largest gap between the documented design and the shipped one, and it is the cheapest to close.

**Fix.** Delete lines 28-33 of `layout.tsx`. The `GeistSans.variable` / `GeistMono.variable` classes on line 26 already define both properties correctly. Then re-check measures and the panel title track, which were tuned against fallback metrics.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`

## [P1] Keyboard focus lands on completely invisible content, in two independent places

**What.** Verified in-browser by both assessments independently.
- The closed mobile nav panel (`site-header.tsx:110`) collapses via `grid-template-rows: 0fr` (globals.css:530-542). At narrow widths it measures 1px tall while its **five links remain focusable** — `link.focus()` succeeds and `document.activeElement` becomes the invisible link. No `inert`, no `aria-hidden`.
- `globals.css:2093-2098` sets `.reveal { opacity: 0 }`. Focusing a `.cite` inside an un-revealed block returns `focused: true, revealOpacity: "0"`.

**Why it matters.** On the primary viewport — a phone — a keyboard or switch user tabbing past the MENY button falls into five invisible navigation links with no indication of where they are. Elsewhere, focus sits on a fully transparent citation link for the 520ms reveal transition, longer if the observer hasn't fired. Both are WCAG 2.4.7 failures on a site whose own PRODUCT.md promises "visible keyboard focus," and this is a campaign that cannot afford to be inaccessible to the public it addresses.

**Fix.** Two lines. Add `inert={!open || undefined}` to the panel div at `site-header.tsx:110`, and add `.reveal:focus-within { opacity: 1; transform: none; }` after globals.css:2098.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

## [P1] Every English page is served as `lang="sv"`

**What.** `layout.tsx:25` hardcodes `<html lang="sv">`. `[lang]/layout.tsx:19-23` patches it with an inline `document.documentElement.lang = "en"` script, which produces a React hydration error on `/en/` (`+ lang="sv" / - lang="en"`). Confirmed in both the dev server and the static export: `out/en/index.html` ships `<html lang="sv"`.

**Why it matters.** This is a static export, so the delivered HTML is wrong for every consumer that parses before scripts run — screen readers announcing English prose with Swedish pronunciation, crawlers indexing the English page as Swedish, translation tooling. PRODUCT.md's accessibility list explicitly requires "correct document language after route selection"; this satisfies it only by client-side patch, and React flags the patch as an error.

**Fix.** Move `lang` into `[lang]/layout.tsx` (return the `<html>` element from the language layout, or use `generateStaticParams` on the root). Delete the inline script.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

## [P2] The stats chapter contradicts DESIGN.md's Four Numbers rule and spends the signal colour on decoration

**What.** globals.css:2592-2649 overrides the spec-correct base implementation at globals.css:741-775. Result: four equal-width cards with vertical dividers, `min-height: 340px`, and gradient accent bars in gold/pink/violet/teal. DESIGN.md §Four Numbers: "not a 2×2 metric dashboard… **Do not use vertical dividers**." DESIGN.md §Colors: gold, rose and violet "never color evidence text or factual status." The fourth bar (2643-2648) uses `--flag`, the colour that means *unanswered* everywhere else. At 820px each card carries ~110px of dead air because `min-height: 340px` holds a row that no longer exists, pushing `.stat__label` 44-72px below its own number — making the number and the sentence explaining it the furthest-apart pair on the page.

**Why it matters.** It is the first module after the hero and the only one an unrelated product could ship unchanged. It hits two of PRODUCT.md's four anti-references (SaaS visual language, attention-grabbing metrics). A reader who sees four differently-coloured bars will look for what the colours encode; nothing does. And by the time they reach `OBESVARAD` in teal, the colour has already been spent as ornament.

**Fix.** Delete globals.css:2592-2649 and let the base horizontal-record implementation stand — it already matches the spec exactly. Delete the dead `.home-stats::after` rules at 2698-2704 and 2722-2724 (no base rule, no `content`, never renders).

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable quieter`

## [P2] The 404 has no chrome, no canvas, and copy naming a control that isn't there

**What.** `not-found.tsx` sits outside `[lang]/layout.tsx`, so it renders with no `.site-frame`, no masthead, no footer — text directly on the raw gold/rose/violet ambient gradient that everywhere else exists *outside* the publication. `document.documentElement.lang` reports `"sv"` while the second paragraph and second button are English with no `lang="en"`. The copy reads "Använd menyn för att gå tillbaka till kampanjen" / "Use the menu to return to the campaign."

**Why it matters.** There is no menu. A reader arriving from a stale shared link — the single most likely way to hit a 404 on a campaign whose conversion is link-sharing — lands on an unbranded gradient, is told to use navigation that does not exist, and sees a full empty gradient band below the fold. It reads as a different, broken site, at the exact moment trust matters most.

**Fix.** Wrap the 404 in `.site-frame` and render `SiteHeader`/`SiteFooter`, or move it to `src/app/[lang]/not-found.tsx`. Rewrite the copy to name the two buttons that actually exist. Add `lang="en"` to the English paragraph and button.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

# Questions to Consider

1. The `STÄLLD`/`SVARET` exchange grid is the only element that makes the reader perform the argument themselves. Why is it confined to one section page instead of being the home page's spine — and what would the four-number chapter look like if each number were an exchange rather than a card?
2. DESIGN.md specifies the Four Numbers module correctly at globals.css:741-775 — then 1,850 lines later a polish pass overrides it into the exact dashboard the document forbids. If the committed design system can be silently overridden by source order, is it a contract or a wish?
3. The site's one semantic colour means "unanswered," and it is spent decoratively on a stat card before the reader has met a single unanswered question. What is the cost of a signal already seen as ornament?
4. PRODUCT.md names sharing as the primary conversion; `page.tsx:74-75` argues persuasively for reading first. Both are right — but the reading path is 9,700px and the share destination is broken. What is the shortest honest path from "I believe this" to "I have posted this," and does it exist?
5. Every source is named, dated and linked — and none is listed anywhere. For an audience of journalists and policymakers, is the absence of a source register a scoping decision or an unexamined assumption that evidence only needs to be reachable, not indexable?
